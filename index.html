For clinicians

Walk in with context, not a cold start.

  • The first fifteen minutes of catch-up become one minute of reading.
  • Signals arrive as patterns over time, not raw transcripts to wade through.
  • You set the care plan; Althio works inside it and reports back to you.

Calm by design. Safe by default.

Althio is a co-therapist, not a replacement. Every deployment sits under a licensed clinician's supervision.

◍

Clinician supervised

Althio never diagnoses or changes a treatment plan. It follows the plan the therapist writes, and the therapist sees its work.

✳

Escalation built in

Crisis signals route to humans immediately — the clinician, the on-call line, or emergency services, by protocol.

◠

Private and consented

HIPAA-eligible infrastructure. Patients choose what is shared, and clinicians see patterns — not private transcripts.
